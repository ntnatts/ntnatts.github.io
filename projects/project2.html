BreathMo® ECMO: Market Entry Strategy

Business Pitch | February 2024

Data Analytics Healthcare Technology Business Strategy

Problem

Advanced ECMO (heart-lung support) technology is not widely accessible in Thailand. Existing imported systems are expensive with no local support. VADDEE needed to validate market opportunity and develop strategy to distribute BreathMo® ECMO to Thai hospitals with growing critical care needs.

Objective

  • Validate market demand for ECMO in Thailand
  • Analyze competitive landscape and positioning
  • Identify and segment target hospitals
  • Design pricing and packaging strategy
  • Project 4-year financial returns
  • Create executable sales roadmap

Scope

  • Market: Thailand (Bangkok entry → 4 regions)
  • Customers: Secondary & teaching hospitals with critical care
  • Product: BreathMo® ECMO system
  • Timeline: 4-year financial projection

Methods

  • Market Research: TAM-SAM-SOM sizing using hospital data, case counts, demand forecasts
  • Competitive Analysis: Feature-price comparison of Getinge, Eurosets, Abbott systems
  • Value Preposition Analysis: Analyse why our product is unique and valuable to the target market

    • Figure 1: Value Proposition Diagram

  • Stakeholder Interviews: Hospital leadership, ICU directors, procurement teams
  • Financial Modeling: 4-year revenue, costs, ROI projections with sensitivity analysis
  • Segmentation Strategy: Tier-1 (Bangkok) → Tier-2 (Regional) phased rollout

    • Figure 2: Teir 1 Hospitals (Bangkok)

    Figure 3: Teir 2 Hospitals (Regional)

Results

  • Market Size: TAM ฿230M → Target (SOM) ฿18M Year 1
  • Demand: 300-500 ECMO cases/year, 10-30% annual growth
  • Customers: 20+ hospitals identified (Bangkok + 4 regions)
  • Competitive Win: MagLev technology superior to Getinge & Eurosets
  • Year 1 Revenue: ฿4.1M | Year 4: ฿28.1M | Total 4-Year: ฿14.3M
  • Financial Returns: 52.81% IRR | ฿7.3M NPV | ฿9.8M Year 4 net income | Breakeven Year 3

    • Figure 4: Break-Even Analysis

  • Strategy: Direct B2B sales via hospitals, conferences, campaigns, outreach

What I Learned

  • Market sizing (TAM-SAM-SOM) is the foundation—assumptions compound into projections
  • Competitive differentiation must solve real customer problems (biocompatibility, portability, cost)
  • Phased geographic expansion (Tier-1 → Tier-2) manages risk and builds capability
  • Recurring revenue models (warranties, service) improve unit economics and customer lifetime value
  • Healthcare sales require simultaneous buy-in from clinicians, administrators, and procurement
  • Strong financial discipline (realistic costs, margin protection) is what drives attractive ROI
  • Data-backed arguments (specific case counts, named hospitals, growth rates) build investor confidence
